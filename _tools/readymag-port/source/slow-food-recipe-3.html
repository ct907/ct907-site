COURSE 3

Overflowing Snack Chirashi Bowl

Prep: As Much As You Want • Cook: As Long As You Need

Total Time: Ehh • Serving: Yes


2 cups cooked Japanese short-grain rice
2-4 tbsp rice vinegar (to your preference)
2 oz sashimi-grade potato chips
2 cucumbers
2 packets of Tic Tacs

2 oz sashimi-grade oreos
2 oz sashimi-grade cheese rings
2 oz cooked nori sheets

3 large eggs

Other assorted snacks (to your preference)
Neutral oil (avocado, canola)
Rice vinegar
Paprika powder
Salt to taste
Mirin to taste
1/4 tsp dashi powder

1 1/2 tsp sugar

1/4 tsp salt

1/2 tsp soy sauce

2 tbsp water



1. PREP

Arrange all your ingredients on your kitchen counter


2. IMPATIENTLY

Check your rice cooker. It should be cooked by now, if not go and Google how long it takes.


3. MIX

Add vinegar, sugar, and salt together then sprinkle over the rice. Stir gently and leave to cool until it is ready to serve.


4. SASHIMI

Combine sashimi grade potato chips, oreos and cheese rings in a bowl. Sprinkle more vinegar and sugar in a bowl. Slather sashimi slices with generous amounts of paprika powder.


5. GREENS

Combine sliced cucumbers, vinegar and sugar. Leave it in the fridge for 2 hours to marinade.


6. TICTACS

Empty 2 packets of Tic Tacs onto a non-stick pan. Toast gently on medium heat until tictacs are golden brown. Cool in a bowl then leave as a topping for later. Prepare your assorted snacks on the kitchen counter.


7. TAMAGOYAKI

Beat eggs well with sugar and salt. Preheat a large pan on medium heat, wipe with oil and then pour in eggs to make a omelet. Once cooked through at about the 1 minute mark, turn off the heat. Roll the eggs onto a plate and once it is room temperature, slice it into julienned strips. Or not. It's up to you.


8. SEASONING

Mix dashi powder, sugar, salt and soy sauce into a bowl. Fill the bottom half of your bowl with 1 cup of rice. Then artfully (unless you're feeling lazy) arrange your chosen ingredients on top and gently spoon a few teaspoons of dressing over any unseasoned toppings.


9. SERVE

Serve the bowl of rice with additional ponzu sauce for dipping if needed. Enjoy!
