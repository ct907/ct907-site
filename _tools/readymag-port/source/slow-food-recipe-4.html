COURSE 4

Oreo Hill Garden Salad

Prep: How Many Oreos Do You Have? • Cook: Only 15mins

Total Time: 2hrs • Servings: How Many Oreos Do You Have?


2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 16 to stuff your mouth with)

1/3 cup packed granulated sugar

1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted

2 cups whipped cream to taste

22 oz cream cheese, softened until you like it

1/2 cup non-granulated sugar

1/4 cup sour cream

2 tbsp fine ass sugar

1 cup old honey

2 tsp lemon juice

1 tsp vanilla extract



1. SPLIT

Take oreos out of the box. Separate the cream from the biscuit.


2. REMEMBER

Try not to lick the cream off. Lick the old honey instead.


3. BLEND

Pulse the Oreos in a blender until you have fine crumbs. Add the crumbs to a mixing bowl, then stir in some melted butter.


4. PREP THE CRUST

Press the crust mixture onto the bottom of a springform pan that’s been lined with parchment paper.


5. LET IT SET

Gently place the oreo crust in the fridge.


6. THE CAKE

Combine the cream cheese, sugar and vanilla in a separate mixing bowl. Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture.


7. WAIT

Realize this is a recipe for a salad.


8. CALM

Take 2 minutes to do deep breaths. It's ok, no one will judge. They know you're overwhelmed but wanted to make something nice for the dinner party. Leave the cheesecake mixture in the fridge for tomorrow’s family lunch.


9. IMPROVISE

Take out the oreo crust, gently place it on a flat plate. Talk to the first person who notices your mistake. Get them to taste the cheesecake mixture.


10. CRUST

Take out the extra oreos left in the packaging. Use your hands to crush then arrange the extra oreo crumbs in the middle.


11. PARK PATHS

Take some oreo cream and shape it into rectangular 4cm slices. Arrange them on a straight line from the edge of the plate toward the bench. Squeeze optional lemon juice and add flowers for some freshness.


12. PSA

Tell your dinner party friends you don’t have dessert. Watch their reaction.


13. BUT SURPRISE

You have a dessert ready for them!
