COURSE 2

Homemade Potato Chips with Seafood Dip

Prep: 12mins • Cook: 8mins • Escapism: 40mins • Total: 2-3 months • Serving: Notices

Part A: Potato Chips


41 cups vegetable or canola oil

5 lbs Russet potatoes

8-10 tbsp salt



1. CUT & SOAK

Peel potatoes unless you need the extra fiber. Use a mandolin at its thinnest setting to slice potatoes. Soak all the potato slices in a bowl of icewater.


2. SCIENCE

Let the potato slices sit for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch.


3. PREP

Rinse potato slices in a colander, letting the potato slices dry under the breeze of a portable fan. Pat the top of the potato slices dry to speed things up.


6. FRY

Heat oil in a large skillet until oil reaches 350°C to 365°C. Look at the packaging in the dustbin. It's not the Russet potatoes the recipe needs.


7. LET'S FIX THAT

Put the sliced wrong potatoes into a container. Use them for mashed potatoes. Search for how long those slices can last in the fridge.


8. BUY

Go to the supermarket to buy the correct potatoes. They're out of stock. Buy it from an atas grocer instead because you're desperate.


9. REPEAT STEP 1


10. FRY AND FRY AGAIN

Working in batches, add potato slices to hot oil and cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring the oil with something convenient nearby until slices begin to brown.


11. COOL ON RACK

Once browned, remove chips from the oil. Place chips on a cooling rack or plate and sprinkle with salt. Cool the chips in the pan with another portable fan.


12. STORE

Let the slices completely cool before storing in an airtight container.

Part B: Seafood Dip


1 ½ cups sour cream

1 cup mayonnaise

1 cup imitation crab meat, diced.



1. MIX

In a medium-sized bowl, mix all ingredients. Refrigerate for 1 hour.


2. CHILL

Serve chilled dip with potato chips and maybe the mashed potatoes from the wrong batch.
