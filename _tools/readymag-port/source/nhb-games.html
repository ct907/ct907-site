Gamifying Heritage Content

To encourage visitors to visit and engage with heritage initiatives, a Pokemon Go style location based gamification project was conceived in 2023.


Players explore heritage locations, check-in and redeem rewards.


This ongoing project grew into a multimodal Gamification Platform with different game modes and reward redemption methods.

Requirements

The MVP for our location check-in webapp pilot was built with these requirements:


1. Fast access: No app store downloads required to participate.

2. Navigate users to an exact location and check-in

3. Dispense rewards and record player progress

Taking inspiration from apps like Pokemon Go and Geocaching, the goal was to encourage players to go outdoors, explore and check-in to heritage areas around Singapore Night Festival while giving players prizes to reward their exploration.


A timeline of 4 months was given to design and build a Pilot Version of the webapp to test during Singapore Night Festival.

Pilot Version

2. EXPLORE


After signing in, players are explore event locations in the map.

1. START PLAYING


In the event site, players scan a QR code from an event signboard or get the game link from the social media/Night Festival website.

3. CHECK-IN


The webapp will alert to notify the player has arrived, and asks the player to Check-in. Checking-in unlocks bonus content like audio guides or fun facts about the site's history and heritage.

4. EARN REWARDS


Once the player has checked-in to a certain number of places, they are able to redeem rewards in the Rewards Page. Staff members will key in a secret passcode to confirm that it has been redeemed. Then the staff member hands the player their gift.

Pilot Results

Being a location-based digital companion and game webapp, it came with its unique set of challenges.


1) To sync up with the event that is hosting the webapp, features like countdown timers were added.

2) Clear navigation hints were needed as event installations or heritage sites may be situated in small street corners or hidden behind vegetation.

3)



The Pilot Test for this webapp was rolled out in the Singapore Night Festival in 2023. Feedback was gathered on site from the reward redemption counters, and visitors who were asking for info from the street team advertising Night Walk.


Due to the fast pace of development, notable issues were seen such as:

1. Check-in locations needing a more obvious indication of check-in status

2. How To Play content being too wordy for a festival visitor context.

3. Having different location subcategories to better organize the check-in locations shown on screen.

Improvements

As the webapp saw success and positive interest in its pilot project, the team expanded the webapp into a reusable platform to accomodate different events and campaigns beyond its initial goal of gamifying Singapore Night Festival.


Plans were underway to grow the CMS frontend for easier Business Unit handoff, and imagining new game formats.

Bonus: NHB Games Mascot

The birth of this new gamification effort from NHB also ushered the creation of a new mascot - Kai


An affable explorer Brahminy Kite, Kai is part of the webapp's development and content strategy under my care.


In the spirit of the webapp, Kai was made to adjust to different events by adjusting to different art styles, while keeping his defining features and mannerisms consistent. Care was taken to make sure his proportions and mannerisms were not lost in translation.


I oversaw the creation of a detailed mascot brand guide, made for future collaborators to bring him to life not only in different events, but also different interactive mediums and game formats in the future.
