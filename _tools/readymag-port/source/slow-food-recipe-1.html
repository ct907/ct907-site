COURSE 1

Coffee Choco Crunchy Bites with Kopi O Kosong+++

Prep: 5mins • Cook: 20mins • Karen Management: 40mins • Total: 2 hrs 45mins • Servings: Too Many

Part A: Coffee Choco Crunch Bites


3 tbsp coconut oil

1 1/2 cup old-fashioned oat flakes
1/3 cup raw honey
3 oz cup cocoa powder for that bitterness
7 tbsp espresso (cold)

14 tbsp salt, no actually 18 tbsp
Vanilla extract, to taste



1. PREP

Grab a large mixing bowl, add all the dry ingredients. Stir to combine.

Add in the coconut oil and honey. Wait impatiently for the mix to clump together.


2. TASTE TEST

If you are reminded of the Dead Sea you're on the right track! Sprinkle 1 more pinch of salt.


3. BAKING PREP

Line non-stick baking paper on a medium-sized tray. Use a small scoop to roll the mixture into small balls.

Take your time, you have a lot of people in the corporate pizza party the next day. Each colleague can eat at least 3 of these.


4. INTO THE OVEN

Bake at 120°C in your office’s pantry toaster oven until the meeting is over.

If the meeting overruns, excuse yourself to check the doneness.
If the meeting keeps going until midnight, it’s probably burnt. Note the total price of the ingredients and claim it as a corporate expense. Retry from Step 1.

Part B: Kopi O Kosong+++


Boiling water
2 oz (double shot) of espresso grounds

Anything else the Karen client wants to add later



1. BREW

Add a double shot of espresso grounds to a tall coffee cup.

Pour your hot water over the coffee grounds.


2. TOPPINGS
Add honey the Karen instructed. Then add 2 tbsp matcha powder. No wait, add strawberry slices. Then also put in a banana. What are you, stupid? Add the banana first, you didn’t listen properly when she was giving instructions.


3. OOPS

In the chaos, watch the cup overfill and drip coffee on the counter.

Then add the honey that Karen said earlier. No, you added too much.

Now she wants to see your manager.


4. HANG TIGHT

Let her tell you how hard her life is and you won’t understand.

Let her finish or she will repeat her story.
Watch her leave the office. She walked through the wrong door and thought she was at a Starbucks.

5. CALM

Get some earplugs. If not, a pair of headphones works fine. Ignore the Karen banging on the office door.

Take a picture of her face on the doorbell camera to report her later.

Pour the completed coffee mixture slowly over the burnt coffee chocolate bites. Enjoy!
