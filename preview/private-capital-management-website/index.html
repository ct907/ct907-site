WEBSITE BUILDING

2021

mAPLETREE PRIVATE CAPITAL MANAGEMENT WEBSITE

New Website for a Private Capital Management Firm

RESPONSIBILITIES


Requirement Gathering, Stakeholder Engagement, Lo-fi - Hi-fi Design Reviews

UX Timeline, IA Analysis and Reorganisation, Design Systems, Developer Handoff, QA Review


OBJECTIVE


Mapletree Private Capital Management wanted a new website to differentiate itself from its larger parent company Mapletree Investments. The clients wanted to showcase their bespoke list of private real estate funds.


Our final design focused on displaying key facts of the company and funds that would entice investors.

We went with a spacious, minimal corporate design to bring focus to their value driven identity,

letting the content speak for itself.


APPROACH


1. Highlighting Mapletree's ESG, which competitors place as an afterthought.

2. Large text cards alongside to highlight key strengths of the funds.

3. Who We Are emphasizes of the company's values and methods of curation.

4. Strategically placed CTAs to lead investors to their Investment Portal.
