MOTION DESIGN

2020

AI DRIVer VISUAL LANGUAGE

Motion Design Based On Speech Act Theory

How would an AI driver present itself when it talks? How does an AI without a face or body visually show emotion or body language?


Part of a larger UX research pilot study on driverless vehicle interactions, the patterns developed here form a visual language used to enhance in-vehicle conversations between passengers and an AI driver.


Final output was used in the pilot study, displayed on the test vehicle’s interior roof surface.

Detection overlays on other vehicles and road users were explored, allowing passengers to see what the AI driver sees.

Based on a visual interpretation of Speech Act Theory, the AI’s visual identity is constructed from modifying glyph-like base patterns.


These base patterns are further augmented by additional framing animations, colors and movement paths based on the emotion and sentence type of the chosen response.
