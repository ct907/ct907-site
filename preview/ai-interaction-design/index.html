AI DRIVER INTERACTION DESIGN

2018

Project Overview

This project was part of the larger ARIA user experience design project to explore how an AI can take agency to alleviate anxiety in driverless rideshare contexts.


With a physical body, human drivers can convey gestures and presence like eyegaze and alertness effortlessly. How would an AI without a physical body be able to do the same using visual means?

Our Distrust In AI

Distrust in AI stems from unfamiliarity of their inner workings. To foster trust, an AI can make use of richer modes of communication to display emotional states or contextual information, similar to facial expressions and body language in humans. Behaviours like mirroring and repetition in human-to-human conversations are used to convey that one is paying attention. For an AI to visually show these kinds of trust building behaviours without a physical body, inspiration from natural phenomena like shadows and glowing algae blooms were explored.

Reusing The Interior

While lacking a physical form, the AI can utilize the interior surfaces of the vehicle to display trust building interactions. Imagining the interior vehicle surfaces as the ‘body’ of the AI creating gestures as it talks, visuals can be projected to let the AI bring about a simulated physical presence.

Paying Attention In Conversations

Taking advantage of the vehicle’s interior surfaces, the AI is able to not only share a space but also have agency in the vehicle’s conversational space with the passenger. Various methods were explored to let the AI fully utilize these display surfaces in conversation.

Final Output

INTEGRATED SURFACE DISPLAYS

The end result is an integrated combination of display surfaces that an AI Driver can use to display more nuanced trip info and bring about a virtual presence to enhance communication with the passenger. This vehicle interior design was used in the larger ARIA user experience design project exploring how an AI can take agency to alleviate anxiety in driverless rideshare contexts.

1. WINDSHIELD SURFACE

An important trust building interaction was to show the AI Driver paying attention to things happening on the road, using an underline overlay to mirror the vehicle’s relative position in space. To convey that the AI driver notices vehicle movement and intention, extra lines are added to show direction and turning.

2. ARMREST SURFACE

For a more private, one to one interaction with the AI Driver, the armrest area was imagined as an additional display surface that only the individual passenger can see. The passenger’s finger positions can be highlighted on the armrest display surface as a mirroring action to show the AI driver is paying attention.

3. WINDOWS

Window surfaces are used for secondary trip information like destinations or non-intrusive advertising.
