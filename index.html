About

Chris Tan is a singer-songwriter and UX designer in Singapore, also known online as ct907. By day, Chris designs digital products, with experience in vehicle AI interaction design and Heritage themed Gamification projects. By night, Chris writes and records chill songs on Spotify.

Music

Slow Food is the second album by Chris Tan: food for the ears, a four-course meal of 12 songs, from Salty to Sweet. Stream Slow Food on Spotify or on all major platforms. Learn more on the Slow Food album page.
