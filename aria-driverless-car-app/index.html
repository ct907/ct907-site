aria autonomous car rideshare app

2018

Project Overview

Can we offer a more personal touch to driverless car rides?

From the initial ride hailing to the journey itself, an AI Driver’s role is to reduce anxiety with the public who are wary of this new technology. The ARIA AI rideshare app design serves as the mobile front end of a larger speculative design project imagining a friendlier and approachable customer experience with these AI Drivers.

Navigating The Unfamiliar

Driverless vehicle adoption is plagued by our unfamiliarity with the technology behind it. A 2017 survey by the American Automobile Association reports that 63% of drivers said they would be afraid to ride in a self-driving vehicle.


In a future when the AI reaches a point of technological maturity, new ways should be explored for the AI to display competence and trustworthiness. While lacking a physical body to convey subtle body language as human drivers would, AI Drivers can use visual means to build trust and alleviate passenger anxiety.

Establishing Trust

In an AI driven rideshare future, the first step of the user journey is mobile first. For passengers new to this experience, the AI Driver would need to convey friendliness and trustworthiness through the ride hailing exchange in a visual manner.


Lo-fi mockups of possible conversations in AI driven rideshare contexts were created. With vast networked knowledge, an AI driver can chat using more nuanced information in its context memory akin to one’s personal driver, serving up recommendations and up to date alerts.

Goals

1. Convey AI friendliness and trustworthiness through conversations

2. Integrate AI Driver’s connected abilities seamlessly

3. Consistent visual look across all of rideshare service AI’s interaction surfaces - Passenger App and Vehicle Interior Displays

Final Output

Visual Appearance

To convey friendliness and reliable performance, a light and clean look was used. Interactions with the AI driver are conversation-based, with the AI residing on a frosted panel on top, reflecting the rideshare vehicle’s windshield and window UI overlays.

Trust and Friendliness

In place of facial features and body language, the AI driver uses hashtags as the subtext to display its thought process as a trust building interaction.

Personalized Interactions

As a human driver would, an AI Driver would find ways to facilitate smooth conversations in multiple ways. The armrest is imagined as an additional interaction surface, allowing opportunies for passengers to have private conversations with the driver in a group setting, akin to leaning over and whispering to your assistant.

Enhanced Network Abilities

An AI driver has access to the connected network, this allows for accurate predictions of complex arrangements. An example is an impromptu meeting room seat configuration to meetup with the upcoming passenger. The enhanced livestream en-route view also provides an opportunity for new passengers to see what the AI sees on the road.
